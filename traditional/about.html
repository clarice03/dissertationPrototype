A NOTE FROM LOWKEY

NO RULES.
NO APPROVAL.
NOTHING TO PROVE.

Style doesn’t need permission. LOWKEY brings together graphic tees, hoodies, cargos, jackets, and accessories for a wardrobe that can move with you and speak in your own voice.

The idea is simple: keep it comfortable, keep it expressive, and make it yours. Wear what feels right. The rest is noise.

ABOUT THIS SITE

A STORE EXPERIENCE,
IN PROTOTYPE.

I’m a Creative Computing student researching how gamification can shape online shopping. This project compares a traditional storefront with a gamified version to explore how interactive features may affect user engagement and the overall shopping experience.

This LOWKEY storefront is a research prototype. Browsing, bag, and checkout interactions are simulated; it does not create accounts, take payments, or place real orders.
